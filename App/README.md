# 🏛️ Real-Time National Land Acquisition & Management System

### SIH 26016 | Team BLAZE

An AI and Blockchain powered platform designed to digitize, streamline, and secure the complete land acquisition lifecycle — from project proposal and document submission to approval, compensation, and final possession.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Land acquisition involves multiple stakeholders, large volumes of documents, approvals, verification processes, and financial transactions.

The proposed system provides a centralized digital platform for managing the complete acquisition workflow while using:

- 🤖 AI for document processing and intelligent validation
- ⛓️ Blockchain for tamper-resistant audit trails
- 🗺️ GIS for land parcel visualization and geo-referencing
- 📄 Digital document management
- 🔄 Workflow-based approvals
- 📊 Real-time status tracking
- 🔐 Secure authentication and authorization

The goal is to make the land acquisition process more transparent, traceable, efficient, and digitally manageable.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

### SIH 26016

**Real Time National Land Acquisition & Management System**

The existing land acquisition process involves multiple authorities and stages, resulting in:

- Manual documentation
- Complex approval workflows
- Difficulty in tracking acquisition status
- Document verification challenges
- Lack of centralized information
- Limited transparency in the workflow
- Difficulty maintaining a reliable audit trail

This project addresses these challenges through a centralized technology-driven platform.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The system provides a unified platform where authorized stakeholders can:

1. Submit land acquisition proposals
2. Upload and manage required documents
3. Process and validate documents using AI
4. Verify land and acquisition information
5. Route applications through defined approval workflows
6. Track acquisition progress in real time
7. Manage compensation-related information
8. Maintain a secure audit trail using Blockchain
9. Monitor land acquisition status through dashboards
10. Manage the complete lifecycle digitally
